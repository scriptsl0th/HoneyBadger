# Analysis

The analysis results live in [RESULTS_AND_ANALYSIS.md](RESULTS_AND_ANALYSIS.md): dataset, method, measured results for objective 1 (supervised classification) and objective 2 (anomaly detection), their interpretation, and what they mean for HoneyBadger.

The notebooks are in `notebooks/` (`intrusion_analysis.ipynb`, `pair1_attack_classification.ipynb`, `pair2_anomaly_detection.ipynb`, `pair3_attack_profiling.ipynb`); the data goes in `data/` and the outputs in `results/`. Reproduce every number with `python scripts/run_analysis.py`.
